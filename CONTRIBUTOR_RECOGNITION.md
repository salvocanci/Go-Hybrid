# Contributor recognition

Public, permanent, and easy to earn. Pick up any role below and it is yours
in the README-visible record. Cash payouts are a separate track with their
own ladder in [BOUNTY.md](BOUNTY.md); this file is about standing.

## Roles

| Role | How you earn it | Perks |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| **First Responder** | Answer someone's question in Discussions, or give feedback that changes the roadmap | Named in the release notes with a link to your reply |
| **Bug Hunter** | First valid soundness report (reproduced, not already known) | Hall of Fame entry, release notes shoutout, `security` all-contributors credit |
| **Security Guardian** | A reproduced Critical or High soundness fix, or sustained quality triage help over a cycle | Hall of Fame entry, invited into the triage rotation, roadmap input on the soundness queue |
| **Builder** | A merged improvement: code, corpus tests, docs, diagnostics, tooling | Hall of Fame entry, `code`/`doc` all-contributors credit |
| **Core Contributor** | Sustained contributions across cycles, trusted judgment | Offered wider ownership: review rights on a subsystem, mentorship of newcomers |

The ladder in one line: report or build, then triage, then regular
contributor, then trusted roles with ownership. We invite people; nobody
has to campaign for it.

## Credit the easy way

Comment on your merged PR or valid issue:

```
@all-contributors please add @yourname for security
```

Swap `security` for `bug`, `code`, `doc`, or `test` to match the work. The
bot opens a PR that adds you to the README's contributors table; approve it
and you are in.

## Where credit shows up

- The Hall of Fame in [BOUNTY.md](BOUNTY.md).
- Release notes for every cycle's winners.
- The README contributors table via All-Contributors.
- Roles and earned standing travel with you: they are per-person, never
  per-report, and they do not expire.

First-timers are the point of this file. A fast, respectful first reply,
credit in the release notes, and a human thank-you are the standard we hold
ourselves to. Be next.
